# 02 — ICP Matrix & Target Segments

*Source: "Ideal Customer Profile Matrix," Avocado Health, August 2026 (confidential), plus July 28 strategy call. Distilled and re-tiered for operational use.*

## 1. Why a matrix and not a persona

Avocado is a B2B2C infrastructure layer. The same platform plugs into at least eight distinct buyer economics — nonprofit grants, government appropriations, provider workforce budgets, payer PMPM contracts, employer PEPM benefits, and owner-operator subscriptions. Each buyer pays for a **different outcome**:

| Buyer pays for | Segments |
|---|---|
| Funding defensibility ("how are you using AI to scale impact?") | CBO, GOV, Foundations |
| Avoided ER / NICU cost | MCO, RHS |
| Staff time saved | FQHC, OB, ABA |
| Workforce productivity | HR |
| Claims reduction | HR, MCO |

Consequence: deal size, pricing model, and sales cycle vary by an **order of magnitude** across segments. Prioritization is therefore mandatory, not optional.

**Headline numbers:** 8 core ICPs + 4 adjacent · ~**$3.0B+** combined near-term addressable spend · **3** Tier-1 segments already have a live named proof point · sales cycles span **1–36 months**.

### Two special cases

**Foundations Supporting Families (Maternal/Child/Parents)**
Program Officer / Senior Program Officer. **Not a direct customer — a connector.** No ACV, no TAM. 3–6 months to relationship. They want to see donation impact scale. Their leverage: they are the ones asking grantees *"how are you leveraging AI to scale impact?"* — which is precisely the pressure that closes CBO and OB deals. **Work them as a channel, never as a prospect.**

**Parenting & Pediatric Content Creators**
Individual creator (+ business manager). High willingness to pay via **50% revenue share**. ACV $25K–$500K, TAM $50–250M, **1–2 month close**. Fastest and highest-variance segment. Sits outside the government thesis entirely — treat as opportunistic, not pipeline-core.

**Private Practice Pediatric Clinics** — listed in the matrix ($5K–$100K ACV, $10M–$25M TAM, 2–4 mo) but **explicitly deprioritized by Hans as the last priority.** Do not source into this segment.

## 3. Buying triggers, objections, and proof points

| Code | Buying trigger | Key objection | Proof point to deploy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OB** | Budget cuts to social workers & child-life specialists; rising maternal mental-health awareness | Grant-dependent funding fragility; integration with existing hotlines/warmlines | Internally flagged as "some of our strongest leads" — frees nurses, doulas, lactation consultants 2–3 hrs/day |
| **ABA** | Staff turnover/burnout; PE roll-up consolidation seeking standardized care | Must be an enhancement, never a replacement, of billable parent-training codes | BCBA owner of 12 clinics endorsement |
| **CBO** | Grant renewal / RFP season; funder pressure to show AI-driven scale | Unpredictable grant cycles; fear tech replaces the human home-visitor relationship | Live white-label with HMHB Hawaii, integrated with Hawaii DOH |
| **HR** | Q3/Q4 open-enrollment planning; rising claims costs | "Another point solution" fatigue; broker gatekeeping; needs engagement data pre-renewal | 60% of workforce are working parents; ~$8K avoided cost/employee at 10–15x ROI |
| **FQHC** | HR1 Medicaid redetermination effective Jan 2027; chronic MA/nurse shortages | EHR integration; thin margins; needs HEDIS-style ROI proof | Documented reduction in repetitive parent inquiries, unnecessary appointments, after-hours MA burden |
| **GOV** | RHTP dollars flowing 2026–2027; Medicaid unwinding workload | Procurement complexity; HIPAA/security review; multi-year budget cycles | Hawaii DOH partnership via HMHB's white-labeled deployment |
| **RHS** | Federal RHTP funding (2026+); rural OB-unit closures | Thin margins; competing capital priorities | Six documented challenge/solution pairs built for rural hospital buyers |
| **MCO** | State redetermination mandates; CMS SDOH/health-equity measures; rising NICU/ER cost trend | Long procurement; needs actuarial-grade ROI before full-book rollout | Positioned against the "$500B Cost of Not Knowing" — preventable preterm births, NICU stays, ER overuse |

## 5. GTM sequencing (company-approved)

**NOW · 0–6 months** — every one already has a live proof point or named testimonial; fast owner-operator or grant-cycle decisions
- Perinatal & Maternal Health Networks (OB)
- ABA & Behavioral Health Clinics (ABA)
- Parent Advocacy Orgs & CBOs (CBO)
- Self-Funded Employers (HR) *— confirm ownership first, see `07` O-5*

**NEXT · 6–12 months** — policy tailwinds mature into RFPs on this horizon
- FQHCs & Rural Clinics
- Rural Hospitals & Health Systems
- State & Local Public Health Departments

**LATER · 12–24 months** — largest contracts, longest cycles; pursue in parallel via warm intros only
- Medicaid MCOs & Payers
- Emerging/adjacent (BRK, API, CW, K12)

## 6. Operating read on the sequencing

The sequencing above is correct but **understates a timing asymmetry worth acting on**:

GOV and RHS sit in "NEXT" because their cycles are 6–18 and 6–24 months. But RHTP money is being programmed by states **now**, in 2026–2027. A 12-month cycle starting in Q4 2026 lands squarely on the funding. **Waiting until month 6 to start GOV sourcing means arriving after the state plans are written.**

Practical resolution: run GOV and RHS **relationship-building** in the NOW window while running OB/ABA/CBO **revenue** in the NOW window. The former is a 2027 revenue play seeded today; the latter is 2026 revenue. Don't let the sequencing chart delay GOV contact mapping — see `04 §3`.

Second read: **CBO and OB are the same motion.** Both are grant-funded, both close in 3–6 months, both are staffed by home visitors / CHWs / doulas whose time Avocado saves, and both are the entities most likely to be the *prime applicant* on a GOV or RHTP opportunity. Treat CBO+OB as one prospecting workstream with two title sets. This is the highest-leverage cell in the entire matrix, because a CBO/OB win is simultaneously a revenue win **and** the vehicle for a GOV win — which is exactly what Hawaii was.
