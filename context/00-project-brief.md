## 1. What this project is

A structured, repeatable **go-to-market and outbound pipeline** for Avocado Health, aimed at the highest-value question the company currently has:

> *"How do we identify the right opportunities — and more importantly, the right people and contacts as target partners/collaborators to win them? Once established, how do we efficiently connect to them?"* — Hans, July 29, 2026

The deliverable is not a one-off list. It is a **standing system**: sourcing → scoring → contact mapping → warm-path → engagement → handoff, with outputs legible enough that leadership can make informed decisions from a dashboard.

## 3. Scope of the internship

**In scope**
- Identify and qualify funding opportunities (RHTP, state DOH, grants, RFPs) where Avocado can be the technology layer
- Identify the **organizations likely to win or administer** those opportunities — these, not the opportunities themselves, are the customers
- Map named decision-makers inside those organizations and find efficient paths to reach them
- Build and maintain the scoring/prioritization system
- Produce a standard, documented outreach process the company can run without Bhuvan

**Out of scope / needs confirmation before touching**
- Employer (HR) and Consultant/Broker tracks — may already be owned internally (see `07`, open question O-5)
- Direct closing or contract negotiation
- Any clinical or medical claims beyond what's documented in `01`

**Explicitly deprioritized by Hans:** individual pediatric clinics. Clinics are the *last* priority, not the first. Government/public health, MCOs, FQHCs, and parent advocacy orgs come first.

## 4. Working rules

1. **Avocado is usually the subcontractor, not the prime.** The Hawaii win was won *by* Healthy Mothers Healthy Babies; Avocado was the platform underneath and let the partner take the credit. Design everything around this. (See `04 §2`.)
2. **The offer is the podcast, not the demo.** Highest-converting top-of-funnel is an invitation onto *The Parent Confidence Lab*. Cold email is the weakest channel. Don't lead with a sales ask.
3. **Never make medical claims.** Avocado provides evidence-based information, not diagnosis or symptom-checking. Scope boundaries in `01 §6` are firm — they are a liability line, not marketing copy.
4. **Cite the source doc.** When producing analysis, note which context doc a claim comes from so it can be verified and updated.
5. **Flag assumptions.** Where data is missing (esp. Civic Operator capabilities, deal history), mark it `[UNCONFIRMED]` rather than filling the gap.
6. **Sensitive material.** MNDA signed Aug 9, 2026. Treat ICP matrix, Riverside brief, HMA report, deal economics, and partner names as confidential. Don't reproduce them in outbound materials.

## 6. Vision (next phase)

A **live dashboard of next steps**: ranked targets with scores, owner, stage, next action, and funding-window countdown — so management sees an informed, standardized outreach plan rather than an ad-hoc list. The scoring model in `04 §4` is designed to be the data layer for that dashboard.
