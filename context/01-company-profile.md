## 1. One-line positioning

Avocado Health is a **"Parent-Coach-in-Your-Pocket"** — a 24/7, SMS-based, hybrid AI + human-expert **parent decision-support platform**, delivered primarily as white-labeled infrastructure for public health agencies, health plans, providers, and community organizations.

**Strategic self-concept:** not a consumer app — a **B2B2C infrastructure layer**. The internal analogy set is Stripe / Shopify / Twilio: invisible to the end user, highly visible to the ecosystem that buys it. (Elaborated in `06`.)

## 3. How the product works

**Delivery**
- Standard **SMS text** and **WhatsApp**. No app download, no portal, no login, no broadband required. ~2 minutes to onboard.
- Rationale: site cites up to **75% of parents won't download a new app**; SMS reaches low-connectivity, rural, and low-digital-literacy populations.

**Intelligence model**
- Domain-constrained AI grounded strictly in **clinician-curated** pediatric, perinatal, and developmental knowledge bases — explicitly *not* a general-purpose chatbot.
- **Anti-hallucination stance:** if a question falls outside confident scope, it says so rather than guessing.
- **Personalization:** retains family history, child age/stage; guidance evolves from pregnancy through the teen years (site frames scope as pregnancy → emerging adulthood).
- **Human-in-the-loop:** escalation to child life specialists, pediatric professionals, parent advocates. Clinical staff have dashboard visibility. Red-flag alerting for critical issues (e.g., postpartum depression).
- **Proactive + reactive:** milestone-timed check-ins and outreach, not just inbound Q&A.

**Data output (the enterprise value)**
- Converts unstructured text into structured **Patient-Generated Health Data (PGHD)**.
- Produces staff-facing longitudinal summaries (**"Family 360 Summary"**).
- Supports billable **Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM)** codes.
- Aggregate, privacy-protected engagement dashboards for partners (sign-up, sustained use, opt-out/pause rates).

**Other capabilities**
- Multilingual — parent texts in Spanish, staff reads/responds in English and vice versa. (Spanish consumer support listed as "launching soon"; multilingual bridging already deployed in partner contexts.)
- **Medicaid eligibility & redetermination** guidance via text — compresses a ~180-day process to ~10–15 minutes (with Liife).
- Weekly live workshops and 1:1s led by pediatricians and child life specialists.
- Ingests a partner's own resources (websites, baby books, clinical guidelines, local food pantry lists, videos) so it speaks in the partner's voice.
- **HIPAA-compliant**; conversations confidential and de-identified.

## 4. The problem framing (use this in proposals)

- Parents make thousands of health and safety decisions **between** brief clinical encounters, usually when trusted guidance is hardest to reach.
- Static educational material doesn't adapt to a child's age or stage; parents default to Google, Instagram, and general-purpose chatbots.
- Consequence: roughly **40% of pediatric ED visits are triaged as non-urgent**, disproportionately affecting rural, low-income, and Medicaid-enrolled families.
- **Parent confidence / self-efficacy** is a leading health indicator that is systematically under-measured and under-supported.

## 5. Proof points (verified, usable in outreach)

### Hawaii — the flagship deployment
Deployed with the **Hawaii Department of Health** through its maternal outreach partner **Healthy Mothers Healthy Babies (HMHB)**, white-labeled as HMHB's **Parent Line**.

- HMHB won a **~$1M/year RFP** from the Hawaii DOH (awarded ~June 2026) to implement the SMS parent support line. **Avocado was the technology underneath; the partner got the credit.**
- Monthly touchpoints went from **~40 calls** on the existing Parent Line to **500+ active touchpoints in the first month**.
- **75%+** active engagement (sends and receives).
- **90%** of SMS read within the first 20 minutes.
- **7** inbound messages/questions per user per month; **12–15** proactive messages delivered per user per month.
- **<$4/month per active covered family** — cost-effective at rural/low-income scale.
- Anecdotal outcomes: reduced postpartum-complication impact incl. PPD, accelerated RSV diagnosis path in infants, improved maternal confidence.
- Positive testimonials from Hawaii DoH leadership, parent advocacy org leaders, and the Parent Line director.
- **Sales cycle: ~12 months** (first contact July 2025 → award June 2026).

### Riverside County / First 5 Riverside (F5RC) — active proposal
Submitted August 2026 for an RHTP collaboration. Key structure worth reusing as a template:
- **Phase 1 pilot** in the most underserved districts — District 4 (Coachella Valley), District 5 (Hemet, San Jacinto, Perris) — before countywide scale.
- **Enrollment through existing touchpoints** (OB practices, midwife/doula programs, CHW programs, HealthySteps sites, home visiting, Family Resource Centers, CA Early Start intake) → **no new hires or clinician workload for the county**.
- **Built against the county's own research.** Cites F5RC's Early Identification & Intervention findings: ~half of surveyed providers named distance a top barrier; **35%** of parents said closer services would most improve their experience; **63%** of expecting parents found no services without a provider pointing the way. Maps directly onto F5RC's stated Opportunity 1 (expand screening/connection) and Opportunity 3 (closed-loop care coordination).
- **43%** of Riverside's population speaks a foreign language at home → multilingual argument.
- **Clinically anchored cadence:** two texts/week, anchored to **gestational week** (not days-to-due-date), from week 6 through 6 weeks postpartum, plus a delivery milestone message. Four content pillars: Clinical, Equity, Emotional, General. Drafted against the ACOG prenatal calendar.
- **Targets the actual drivers of maternal morbidity:** hypertensive disorders/preeclampsia (wk 10, wk 14, wk 32–35, postpartum wk 1 & 4), hemorrhage (first days postpartum), gestational diabetes (glucose challenge reminder wk 24–27), perinatal mental health (repeat depression screen wk 28–31, PPD normalization postpartum wk 2).
- **Safety rails:** one-tap pause with **no automated re-engagement after pregnancy loss**, bereavement resource routing, same-day escalation language for red flags. This is a differentiator — lead with it when partners raise clinical-discretion concerns.

## 6. Scope boundaries — the liability line

**Firm rules. These are not marketing choices.**

- Medical diagnosis and symptom-checking must come from licensed providers. Avocado provides **evidence-based information**, not diagnostic advice.
- **No decision-tree symptom checkers** — that crosses into diagnosis.
- Clinical staff stay in the background with dashboard visibility; red-flag alerting routes to humans.
- Public-facing scope of at-home triage support: common cold, mild fever, minor cuts, mild diarrhea/rash, insect bites, bumps/bruises. Anything serious is explicitly directed to **911** / the family's provider.
- For ABA and clinical partners, Avocado must always be positioned as an **enhancement to**, never a replacement for, billable services.
