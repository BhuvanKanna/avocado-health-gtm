Strongest Opportunity Matches

1Idaho RHTP School-Based Family Support Hubs

Sponsoring organization. Idaho Department of Health and Welfare.
Geographic scope. Eligible rural Idaho school districts.
Status and dates. Posted August 11, 2026. Applications due September 8, 2026. RFPs item 12890246703 is Verified as of September 3, 2026.

Why it matches. The solicitation funds physical school-based family support hubs in rural communities underserved by outpatient mental and behavioral health clinics. Services made available through the hubs may include mental and behavioral health support, family therapy, parent education, and workshops. The category fit is direct, but the commercial route is indirect: eligible school districts are the applicants, and the solicitation permits partnerships and, at district discretion, subgrants to nonprofit or community-based organizations.

2Missouri RHTP Request for Information — Virtual Care Expansion

Sponsoring organization. Missouri Department of Social Services, Rural Health Transformation Office.
Geographic scope. Rural Missouri, statewide.
Status and dates. Posted and open as of a read of the state program page on September 3, 2026. Responses due September 9, 2026. No time of day or timezone is published on the state page.

Why it matches. The RFI is organized around two tracks, the first titled Remote Patient Monitoring for Maternal Care and the second Telehealth Specialist Access for Rural Hospitals and Providers. The state is soliciting vendor input before it has specified a model: the page states the RFI "seeks input from vendors to help shape two distinct tracks," and asks respondents to address available system capabilities, integration with existing systems, technical and security requirements, implementation approaches, pricing, and ongoing support. Respondents may answer one track or file separately for both.

Source. https://dss.mo.gov/mhd/rural-health

Contacts. Steven Hoffman (Steven.Hoffman@dss.mo.gov), named RFI point of contact, no title published; DSS.RHTP@dss.mo.gov, general program inbox. Both in the contact table below.

3Kentucky RHTP First-Tier Subaward Cohort

Sponsoring organization. Kentucky Cabinet for Health and Family Services.
Geographic scope. Statewide Kentucky; sixteen of the 19 human-verified recipients are county or district health departments.
Status and dates. Nineteen Verified first-tier subaward obligations, every action dated July 1, 2026.

Why it matches. Local health departments are a named buyer type in the rubric's operating notes. Kentucky's Rural Health Transformation Plan separately identifies PoWERing Maternal and Infant Health, which expands prenatal and postpartum access through telehealth-enabled maternal care teams and reports that one in six Kentucky women of childbearing age lives in a maternity care desert. Sixteen of the 19 verified first-tier subaward recipients are county or district health departments. The subaward records do not assign those recipients to PoWER or any other named initiative, so the cohort supports buyer fit but not a verified maternal-program linkage.
